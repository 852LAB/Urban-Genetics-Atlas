Welcome to the Urban Genetics Atlas
Integrated Urban Analysis by 852LAB

The Urban Genetics Atlas brings together a range of spatial datasets to explore the structure, condition and transformation potential of Hong Kong's urban fabric.

The Atlas is an analytical and exploratory tool rather than a complete or definitive representation of the city. The underlying data is compiled from multiple sources, with differing levels of coverage, detail and completeness.

As a result, gaps, missing values, outliers and extreme observations are present. Data preparation and analysis have sought to identify and minimise these effects where practical, but they cannot be eliminated entirely.

The Atlas should therefore be used to identify patterns, relationships and emerging trends, rather than interpreted as a precise statement about any individual location.

The intention is to provide a framework for questioning how the city is structured, how it is changing, and where further investigation may be warranted.
