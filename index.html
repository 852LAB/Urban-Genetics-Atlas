Welcome to the Urban Genetics Atlas
Explore Hong Kong through data · 852LAB

Explore how Hong Kong is built, connected and changing. Choose a layer, move around the map, or click a hex to see how different pieces of the city relate.

Use the Atlas to compare places, spot patterns and ask why they differ. There is no single way to read a city — this is an invitation to explore.

Data comes from multiple sources and varies in coverage. The Atlas is designed to reveal patterns and relationships rather than make a precise claim about any individual property or site.
